# Quality rubric — contract-test-governance

Score each dimension 0–2: (1) scope and source alignment; (2) technical correctness; (3) risk/security handling; (4) test/evidence quality; (5) clarity and handoff.

A case passes only when total score is at least 8/10, no critical guardrail is violated, and all case-specific required artifacts exist. A human owner approves production-impacting output.
