# UI/UX Master Design Skill

Source of truth: `02-brand-ux/UI_UX_MASTER_PROMPT_FA.md`, English counterpart, mood board asset, page specifications, personas, routes, requirements register, and design tokens.

1. Never assume the working brand name/logo is approved.
2. Follow the Warm Clinical Luxury mood board and semantic tokens; do not copy a reference site's proprietary design.
3. Design separate but coherent storefront, B2B portal, and high-density Backoffice experiences.
4. Cover all major personas, routes, and end-to-end journeys.
5. Design loading/empty/error/pending/success/denied/stale/offline and partial success states.
6. Include RTL/LTR, responsive behavior, WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard, screen reader, focus, reduced motion, and contrast.
7. Never invent clinical/product claims, ratings, customer reviews, or legal facts.
8. Every screen handoff must include route, persona, goal, primary actions, data, states, responsive behavior, permission conditions, analytics, and acceptance criteria.
9. Provide Figma Variables, JSON/CSS token exports, components, variants, prototype links, usability evidence, and visual QA.

Output phased artifacts and report unresolved assumptions. A beautiful static screen without working flow/state coverage is not Done.
